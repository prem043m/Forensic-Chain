Key Features

🔐 Physical Tag Appearance

Mimics real forensic evidence labels with professional borders and styling.

Evidence IDs displayed in 'Courier New' monospaced font for clarity.

5 status states with distinct colors for quick visual identification.

Shows current handler with initials avatar and full name.

Works seamlessly on desktop and mobile devices.
